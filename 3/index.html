3Gellért Hill & the Citadella

Gellért-hegy

Wooded climb to the newly reopened fortress and Liberty Statue – the postcard view, at sunset.

4.6Google · 23.2k reviews

Directions

Why it's on the menu

The postcard view of Budapest – every bridge, Parliament and the Castle laid out below you – from a wooded hill right in the middle of the city. The Citadella fortress on top reopened this year after a long renovation, with a new park and terraces around the Liberty Statue. Time it for sunset and stay as the lights come on.

What to do

  • Walk up from the Buda end of Elisabeth Bridge, past the St Gellért statue and through the woods (about 30 min).
  • Walk the Citadella bastions and the Liberty Statue terrace – the park is free.
  • Optional: the new history exhibition and rooftop terrace (10–18, about 4,900 HUF).
  • Watch the sunset (around 16:35) and the city lighting up.

What reviewers say

The panorama over the Danube and its bridges is the best in the city.Google reviewers (paraphrased)

Good to know

  • Be at the top by about 16:15 for sunset.
  • Down: the stairs on the Liberty Statue side to Szent Gellért tér, then tram 47/49 to Astoria and the M2 one stop home.
  • The Buda end of Elisabeth Bridge has roadworks – check the bus in the BudapestGO app.
