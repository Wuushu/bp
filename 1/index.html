Why it's on the menu

The best day out of the city: a train up the Danube, a little ferry across the river, then a proper hike up to a 13th-century hilltop castle with the classic view over the Danube Bend – where the river swings between wooded hills. History, a real climb and a huge view in one day, and you're back in Budapest in time for dinner.

What to do

  • Train from Nyugati station to Nagymaros-Visegrád (about 40 min), then the small ferry across (10 min, every hour).
  • Hike up the blue-marked path past the Calvary chapel to the castle (Fellegvár), about 45 minutes.
  • Walk the castle walls for the Danube Bend panorama, then lunch at Silvanus Panoráma, 5 minutes away.
  • On the way down: the Royal Palace ruins on the main street (closed Mondays).

Good to know

  • Steep, stony path after the chapel – wear proper shoes.
  • From Tuesday the castle closes at 16:00. Ferries back leave Visegrád at :45 past each hour – the 15:45 gets you to the hotel around 17:05.
  • No Danube boats run this autumn (low water) – train plus ferry is the way.
  • A Pest county day ticket (999 HUF in the MÁV app) covers the train; the ferry is paid separately (~950 HUF).
