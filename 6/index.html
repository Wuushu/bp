6Normafa & the chairlift

Normafa · Libegő

Forest walk to Budapest's highest point, then float down over the treetops by chairlift.

4.7Google · chairlift · 8.2k reviews

Why it's on the menu

The Buda Hills classic: a meadow at the edge of the forest, a walk up to the city's highest point (527 m), then a slow, silent chairlift ride down over the treetops. More forest than city views, but a lovely autumn half-day and something different.

What to do

  • Bus 21 up to Normafa – grab a strudel at the pavilions.
  • Forest walk to János-hegy, the highest point, and the terrace at the chairlift top.
  • Ride the Libegő chairlift down (about 12 min), then bus 291 back towards the centre.

What reviewers say

Pay one way and walk the other – the ride down is the highlight.Google reviewers · chairlift (paraphrased)

Good to know

  • The chairlift is closed on Mondays.
  • The Elizabeth Lookout tower on top is closed for repairs – use the chairlift-top terrace instead.
  • Chairlift about 3,000 HUF one way.
