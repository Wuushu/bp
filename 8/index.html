8Füvészkert botanical garden

ELTE Füvészkert

Hungary's oldest botanical garden (1771): a 19th-century palm house, orchids, cacti and more.

Why it's on the menu

Hungary's oldest botanical garden, founded in 1771 and run by ELTE University – a walled green oasis about 30 minutes' walk from the hotel. The heart of it is a 19th-century glass palm house, plus houses for orchids, cacti and carnivorous plants, old trees, ponds and even its own small Japanese garden. Hungarians know it from 'The Paul Street Boys', the classic novel whose rival gang meets here. Glasshouses make it good in any weather.

What to do

  • Start with the great Palm House, then the orchid, cactus and carnivorous-plant houses.
  • Walk the outdoor beds, the pond and the small Japanese garden.
  • Look for the oldest trees – some date from the garden's early days.

Good to know

  • Open daily 9–17; the ticket office closes at 16:30.
  • Tickets about 2,200 HUF (seniors 1,300 HUF).
  • The glasshouses occasionally close for a few hours for plant spraying.
  • Entrance at Illés utca 25. Tram 4/6 to Corvin-negyed, then about 15 minutes along Üllői út.
