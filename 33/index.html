Why it's on the menu

The one proper Hungarian dinner: a family-run vendéglő (traditional restaurant) since 1996, named Budapest's best Hungarian restaurant by Time Out in 2025 and freshly renovated this autumn. It's warm and unpretentious rather than white-tablecloth, most regulars are Hungarian, and the kitchen does the classics – goose, stuffed cabbage, game – with generous portions. A good place to linger over a long dinner.

What to do

  • Start with a soup – the tarragon venison soup or a fisherman's soup.
  • Main: roast goose leg or stuffed cabbage (töltött káposzta).
  • Dessert: flódni (a layered Jewish-Hungarian cake) or a flaming palacsinta.

Good to know

  • Closed on Sundays. Open Monday–Saturday 12–23.
  • About 11,000–15,000 HUF per person for soup, main, dessert and a soft drink.
  • 8 minutes by taxi from the hotel, or M2 one stop to Keleti and a 5-minute walk.
  • Backup if it's full: Gettó Gulyás (no. 29).
