Why it's on the menu

Real crimes in the lamp-lit Castle District, told by a guide dressed as a Victorian detective – with the Danube glittering below. A smaller group than the big castle night tours.

When
Evening · 20:00 – 22:00

~6,500 HUF each (€18)

What to do

  • Meet at Szent György tér at 20:00.
  • About 2 hours of stories through the Castle District.
  • Ends at Clark Ádám tér by the Chain Bridge.

Good to know

  • Max 30 people. Called off in heavy rain (new date or refund). Ages 18+.
  • About 30 minutes each way – tram 4/6 + bus 16, or a 15-minute taxi.
