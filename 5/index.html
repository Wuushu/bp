Why it's on the menu

A quiet, local side of Buda that most visitors never see: steep cobbled lanes between villas, a 16th-century Ottoman tomb with a rose garden and a terrace over the Danube, and two little-known viewpoints. A short morning walk with city views and history, and an old 1960s café at the bottom.

What to do

  • Walk up steep Gül Baba utca to the tomb of Gül Baba and its rose-garden terrace.
  • Continue through the villa streets to Mansfeld Péter park and the József-hegy stone lookout.
  • Coffee, toasties or cake at Bambi Eszpresszó on the way down.

Good to know

  • The tomb is closed on Mondays; entry is free.
  • Tram 4 or 6 straight from the hotel to Margit híd (Buda side), about 15 minutes.
  • About 3 km with some steep cobbles – comfortable shoes.
