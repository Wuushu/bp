2Gödöllő – Sisi's palace

Gödöllői Királyi Kastély

Empress Elisabeth's favourite baroque palace: restored royal rooms and a quiet park.

4.6Google · 15.5k reviews

Why it's on the menu

Empress Elisabeth – Sisi – loved this baroque country palace more than any other Habsburg residence, and it's where she felt most at home in Hungary. You walk through her restored rooms and the royal apartments, then out into a quiet park. It's the rainy-day trip, and it's open on Monday when much of Budapest's culture is closed.

What to do

  • The royal apartments of Franz Joseph and Sisi, with portraits and her personal rooms.
  • The baroque ceremonial hall and chapel.
  • A walk in the palace park, then coffee or lunch at Café Ida on the ground floor (no ticket needed).

What reviewers say

Even if you've seen other Sisi palaces, this one – with its garden and story – is worth the trip.Google reviewers (paraphrased)

Good to know

  • Open daily 10–18, last tickets at 17:00.
  • M2 from Blaha Lujza tér to Örs vezér tere (10 min), then the H8 suburban train to Gödöllő Szabadság tér (about 45 min). The palace is across the road.
  • About 5,000 HUF on weekdays, a little more at the weekend.
