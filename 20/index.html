Tóth Árpád sétány

Chestnut-lined rampart walk facing the sunset, palace terraces, then down to the lit-up Danube.

4.6Google · Várkert Bazár · 10.3k reviews
Open in Google Maps
Directions

Why it's on the menu

The quietest way to see the Castle: a chestnut-lined promenade along the western walls that faces the sunset, then the palace terraces, and down through the Várkert Bazár gardens to the Danube as the lights come on.

What to do

  • Walk up Várfok utca to Vienna Gate.
  • Follow Tóth Árpád sétány along the walls in the golden hour.
  • Cross to the palace terraces for the Parliament view, then take the free escalators down to the river.
