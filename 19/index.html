Why it's on the menu

The famous 1897 market hall is touristy, but the building is magnificent: a vast iron hall under a colourful Zsolnay tile roof. Worth 45 minutes for the architecture and a look down from the gallery – just don't eat upstairs.

What to do

  • Ground floor: fruit, vegetables, salami and paprika stalls.
  • Basement: pickles and the fish market.
  • Gallery: the view over the whole hall.

Good to know

  • Upstairs food is tourist-priced – eat elsewhere.
  • Compare prices between stalls, count your change, and buy paprika in sealed bags on the ground floor.
  • Sunday 10–16 with many stalls shut; weekday mornings are best.
  • 10 min walk to Rákóczi tér, then M4 two stops to Fővám tér.
