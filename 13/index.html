13St Stephen's Basilica dome

Szent István-bazilika

Lift and stairs up the 96 m dome to a 360° terrace over the rooftops. Best in late light.

4.7Google · 81.3k reviews
Open in Google Maps
Directions

Why it's on the menu

Budapest's largest church has a lift and stairs up to a terrace running all the way around the 96 m dome – the best rooftop view on the Pest side, and it stays open until 19:00, so it works even late in the day.

What to do

  • The 360° terrace around the dome – go in the late afternoon for golden light.
  • Inside: the gilded nave and the dome seen from below.
  • Optional: the treasury, included with the terrace ticket.

What reviewers say

The panoramic view is very worth it – look for the small lift, it's easy to miss.Google reviewers (paraphrased)

Good to know

  • Terrace open daily 9–19; the church interior opens only from 13:00 on Sundays.
  • Terrace about 5,000 HUF; with the interior about 6,800 HUF.
  • 20 minutes' walk from the hotel, or M1 from Oktogon to Bajcsy-Zsilinszky út.
