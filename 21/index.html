Why it's on the menu

Budapest's most beautiful walk when the lights come on: Parliament glowing on the water, the Shoes on the Danube memorial, the Chain Bridge and the Castle lit up across the river – all flat, about an hour.

What to do

  • Start at Parliament (Kossuth Lajos tér).
  • The Shoes on the Danube memorial to the Jews shot here in 1944–45.
  • Past the Chain Bridge to Vigadó tér and Elisabeth Bridge.

What reviewers say

One of the most powerful memorials in Budapest.Google reviewers · Shoes on the Danube (paraphrased)

Good to know

  • The lower quay is car-free at weekends.
  • Don't eat on the promenade or Váci utca – bread cover charges and inflated prices.
  • M2 to Kossuth tér (10 min); home from Vörösmarty tér on the M1.
