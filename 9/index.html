Why it's on the menu

Miklós Ybl's 1880s garden terraces at the foot of the Royal Palace, fully restored: arcades, a Neo-Renaissance garden with rose trees, boxwood parterres and the Triton fountain, and benches looking out over the Danube to Parliament. The gardens are free and open until midnight, and a free escalator and lift lead up to the palace – so it's the gentle way into the Castle District.

What to do

  • Walk in from Ybl Miklós tér on the riverside and through the arcades.
  • The Neo-Renaissance garden: rose trees, boxwood and the Triton fountain.
  • Up the ramps to the Gloriett and the terraces for the Danube and Parliament view.
  • Take the escalator or lift up to the palace courtyards, a few minutes away.

Good to know

  • Gardens open daily 6–24, free. Exhibitions inside are Tuesday–Sunday and ticketed.
  • Bus 16 from Deák tér to Clark Ádám tér, then 5 minutes along the river.
  • Weekend lunch: VakVarjú VarjúVár is right on site (★ 4.8), but many reviews name their waiter – a sign they're prompted, so treat the rating with care.
  • If the gate is open, look for the small Turkish garden of figs, quinces and roses by the Karakas Pasha Tower on the steps up to the palace.
