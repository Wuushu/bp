15Wenckheim Palace library

Szabó Ervin Könyvtár

A public library inside a neo-baroque palace: the ballroom is now a hushed reading room.

4.6Google · 1.9k reviews
Open in Google Maps
Directions

Why it's on the menu

A secret most visitors miss: Budapest's central library lives inside a neo-baroque aristocrat's palace. The former ballroom, smoking room and salons are now quiet reading rooms – chandeliers, gilding and wood panelling, with students working at the tables. Short, free of crowds, and close to the Great Market Hall.

What to do

  • Buy a day ticket at the entrance desk.
  • Go up to the 4th-floor palace rooms: the ballroom, the smoking room and the spiral library stair.
  • Combine with the Great Market Hall, 10 minutes' walk.

Good to know

  • Closed this weekend; open Monday–Wednesday.
  • It's a working library – keep voices down and no flash.
  • Day ticket about 1,700 HUF. 20 minutes' walk to Kálvin tér, or M4 from Rákóczi tér.
