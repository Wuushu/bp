Why it's on the menu

One of Europe's most beautiful opera houses, fully restored: gold, red velvet and painted ceilings. The guided tour shows you the grand staircase, the royal box and the auditorium, and ends with a short live performance.

What to do

  • English tour at 13:30, 15:00 or 16:30 (about 1 hour).
  • The grand staircase, the smoking salon, the royal box and the auditorium.
  • A short live performance at the end.

Good to know

  • Tickets go on sale one week ahead online; about 12,000 HUF.
  • On Sunday the 13:30 tour may be dropped for a matinee – pick a later one.
  • 12 minutes' walk along Andrássy út from Oktogon.
