10Andrássy út to City Park

Andrássy út · Városliget

Grand boulevard to Heroes' Square, fairy-tale Vajdahunyad Castle and the Millennium rose garden.

Why it's on the menu

Grand Budapest on foot: the tree-lined Andrássy út boulevard with its villas and the Opera, the colonnades of Heroes' Square, then City Park – fairy-tale Vajdahunyad Castle on its lake, and for the gardeners, the rose garden with its Zsolnay fountain by the Millennium House.

What to do

  • Walk Andrássy út from Oktogon to Heroes' Square.
  • Cross to Vajdahunyad Castle on its little lake.
  • Find the rose garden and Zsolnay fountain by the Millennium House (Olof Palme sétány).
  • Walk up onto the free green roof of the new Museum of Ethnography for the view.

What reviewers say

A beautiful, impressive square – the start of a lovely walk into the park.Google reviewers · Heroes' Square (paraphrased)

Good to know

  • Museums are closed on Mondays (the walk itself is fine).
  • A pumpkin festival runs by the ice rink this week.
  • Home on the M1 from Hősök tere to Oktogon.
