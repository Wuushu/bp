16New York Café – just for the room

New York Kávéház

The gilded 1894 café called the world's most beautiful, 7 min away. Coffee and cake only.

4.1Google · 70k reviews ⚠ CAUTION

Tourist-priced; go 7–9 am or after 22:00 to skip the queue

Why it's on the menu

Often called the world's most beautiful café: an 1894 hall of marble, gilding, frescoes and chandeliers, a 7-minute walk from the hotel. It's on the list for the room itself – have a coffee and a slice of cake, not a meal.

What to do

  • Coffee and a classic cake: Dobos torte or Esterházy torte.
  • Look up – the painted ceilings are the point.
  • Go early (7–9) or late (after 22) to skip the queue.

What reviewers say

Stunning room, but queues and slow service spoil it for some.Google reviewers (paraphrased)

Good to know

  • Tourist-priced: about 9,500 HUF each for coffee and cake, including a 15% service charge.
  • Breakfast can be booked in October; otherwise reservations are mostly for dinner.
