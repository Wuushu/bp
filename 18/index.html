18Lehel Market – the locals' one

Lehel Csarnok

Where Budapest actually shops: butchers, paprika, pickles and old-school lunch counters.

Why it's on the menu

The market where Budapest actually shops: butchers, paprika, pickles, cheese and old-school lunch counters, and almost no tourists. The ship-shaped building is more quirky than grand, but the food is real, cheap and good.

What to do

  • Walk the lower-level stalls: sausages, cheese, pickles, paprika.
  • Eat a fresh lángos (fried flatbread with sour cream and cheese) at Lángos centRum on the lower level.
  • Pick up paprika and Túró Rudi to bring home.

Good to know

  • Sunday only until 13:00; Monday and Tuesday are the quietest days.
  • Some renovation scaffolding, but the market stays open.
  • Tram 4/6 to Nyugati, then M3 one stop to Lehel tér (about 20 min).
