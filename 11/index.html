Why it's on the menu

Budapest's grandest interior, and the one building worth seeing from the inside if you only pick one. The tour takes you up the gilded grand staircase to the Holy Crown of Hungary, guarded under the 16-sided dome, and into the old Upper House chamber with its carved wood and red velvet. It's short, guided, and pairs perfectly with a sunset walk along the Danube right outside.

What to do

  • 45-minute guided tour in English, starting from the underground visitor centre.
  • The golden main staircase, the dome hall with the Holy Crown (no photos there), the old Lords' chamber.
  • Afterwards: walk south along the river to the Shoes on the Danube memorial (5 minutes) as the lights come on.

Good to know

  • Arrive 20 minutes early with a passport or national ID card – there is airport-style security.
  • EU citizens pay about 7,000 HUF; English tours sell out, so book ahead.
  • M2 from Blaha Lujza tér to Kossuth Lajos tér, about 10 minutes.
