Why it's on the menu

A small-group walk (max 10) with a local through markets and street-food stops: a farmers' market, a butcher's roastery, gulyás in a tavern, lángos and chimney cake. Lunch is included many times over.

What to do

  • A farmers' market with cheese and sausage tastings.
  • A butcher's roastery and a traditional tavern for gulyás.
  • Lángos and kürtőskalács (chimney cake) to finish.
