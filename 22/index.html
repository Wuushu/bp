22Kerepesi Cemetery at dusk

Fiumei úti sírkert

Plane-tree avenues and grand mausoleums of Hungary's great names; first All Saints candles.

Why it's on the menu

Budapest's grandest cemetery: 56 hectares of plane-tree avenues and huge mausoleums for Hungary's great names. In the week before All Saints the first candles appear – atmospheric, peaceful and full of sculpture.

What to do

  • Walk the main avenue to the Batthyány and Deák mausoleums.
  • The domed Kossuth mausoleum with its mosaics.
  • Wander the arcades of the old family tombs.

What reviewers say

The tombstones are works of art – a quiet, peaceful place.Google reviewers (paraphrased)

Good to know

  • Open until 20:00 from Monday; free entry.
  • Families visit graves this week – be discreet. No café inside.
  • 25 min walk, or M2 one stop to Keleti plus 8 minutes.
