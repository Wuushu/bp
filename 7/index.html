7Margaret Island & the Japanese Garden

Margitsziget · Japánkert

Car-free island park: a rose garden, convent ruins and a 1930s Japanese garden with koi and turtles.

Why it's on the menu

A car-free island park in the middle of the Danube, two tram stops from the hotel – Budapest's favourite place to stroll. For two gardeners it strings together three gardens: the big rose garden in the middle, the ruins of the 13th-century convent where St Margaret lived, and at the north end a small 1930s Japanese garden with a pond, a waterfall, koi, water lilies and turtles. Quiet on a late-October weekday, with autumn colour all round.

What to do

  • Tram 4/6 to the Margitsziget stop in the middle of Margaret Bridge and walk onto the island.
  • Walk north along the main path: the rose garden, the convent ruins and the Art Nouveau water tower.
  • At the north end: the Japanese Garden – pond, rock garden, waterfall, the 'Sitting Girl' statue – and the Musical Well next door.
  • Bus 26 runs back down the island to the bridge, or walk back along the river side.

Good to know

  • Free, and open all day. About 4 km if you walk the island one way.
  • Toilets and a few kiosks on the island; little else to eat out of season – lunch is better on the Pest side.
  • Bonus for gardeners: two minutes from the lunch café, Szent István park has a small rose garden of Hungarian-bred varieties.
