Why it's on the menu

The heart of old Buda: Matthias Church with its diamond-patterned tiled roof and painted interior, the fairy-tale white turrets of Fisherman's Bastion, and the palace terraces looking straight across the river at Parliament. Monday is ideal – it's busy at weekends and the church only opens in the afternoon on Sunday.

What to do

  • Matthias Church – go inside; the painted interior is as striking as the roof (about 3,400 HUF).
  • Fisherman's Bastion terraces – the lower ones are always free.
  • The changing of the guard at Sándor Palace, on the hour; the big one is at 12:00.
  • Optional: Hospital in the Rock, a Cold War hospital and bunker inside the hill (hourly tours).

Good to know

  • Tram 4/6 to Széll Kálmán tér (20 min), then walk up Várfok utca to Vienna Gate.
  • Down: the free escalators and lift at Várkert Bazár to the riverside.
  • Skip the restaurants on Holy Trinity Square – you pay for the view. Lunch at the foot of the hill instead.
