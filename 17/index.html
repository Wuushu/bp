Why it's on the menu

Budapest sits on a maze of caves. This guided hour takes you through narrow dripstone passages deep under the Buda Hills – a little adventurous, very atmospheric, and a good rainy-day alternative that pairs with Three Border Hill on the same bus line.

What to do

  • Tours leave every hour, 10:15–16:15, and take about an hour.
  • Squeeze through narrow passages and up 400 steps and a ladder – fun rather than hard.
  • Combine with the Three Border Hill hike (bus 65).

What reviewers say

Very interesting – bring extra layers, it's cold. About 45 minutes, so not too long.Google reviewers (paraphrased)

Good to know

  • Closed on Mondays.
  • About 3,900 HUF; commentary through an English audio app.
  • Tram 4/6 + tram 17 to Kolosy tér, then bus 65 (about 40 min).
