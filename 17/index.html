17Chef Andrew's private market tour

Why it's on the menu

Two hours, just the two of you, with a local former chef who explains what Hungarians actually eat – tasting as you go. It's the closest thing Budapest has to a 'supermarket tour': the snacks and staples locals grew up on, and honest advice on what's worth buying.

What to do

  • Tastings of lángos, strudel, sausages, pogácsa, crackling and pickles.
  • The Hungarian supermarket classics – Túró Rudi and friends.
  • Tips on what to bring home and what to skip.

What reviewers say

He steered us away from a poor-quality purchase – no pushy selling at all.Viator / Tripadvisor reviewers (paraphrased)

Good to know

  • Private tour, about 2 hours. Choose the Great Market Hall or the more local Lehel Market when booking.
  • Listed at 09:00 – ask for a 10:00 start.
  • The pálinka shot is optional; also skip the cognac cherry.
